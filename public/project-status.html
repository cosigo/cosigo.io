COSIGO educational purpose and project status

COSIGO™ is primarily a privately funded educational and experimental project. It demonstrates how digital wallets, blockchain records, person-to-person transfers, market-access tokens, liquidity pools, servers, verification infrastructure, and related technology work in practice.

Learning through a working demonstration

The COSIGO websites, the separate CIGO token, liquidity pool, swap interfaces, server infrastructure, and public market information provide a working environment for observation and practical learning. Their existence does not mean COSIGO is presently operated as a revenue-producing business or that it is offering an investment.
